# Coffee-Sales-Analysis


## Project Overview

This project analyses online coffee sales across different cities in India using SQL and Power BI.
The main objective is to identify the three most promising cities for opening physical coffee shops.
## Questions
- Q.1 Total Revenue from Coffee Sales, What is the total revenue generated from coffee sales across all cities in 2023?
- Q.2 sales Count for Each Product, How many units of each coffee product have been sold?
- Q.3 Average Sales Amount per City, What is the average sales amount per customer in each city?
- Q.4 City Population and Coffee Consumers (25%),Provide a list of cities along with their populations and estimated coffee consumers.
- Q5 Top Selling Products by City, What are the top 3 selling products in each city based on sales volume?
- Q.6 Customer Segmentation by City, How many unique customers are there in each city who have purchased coffee products?
- Q.7 Average Sale vs Rent, Find each city and their average sale per customer and avg rent per customer
- Q.8 Monthly Sales Growth, Sales growth rate: Calculate the percentage growth (or decline) in sales over different time periods (monthly) by each city
- Q.9 Market Potential Analysis, Identify top 3 city based on highest sales, return city name, total sale, total rent, total customers.

## Business Recommendations – Top 3 Cities for Expansion

Based on the analysis of online coffee sales, customer activity, and estimated rent per customer, 
I recommend Pune, Chennai, and Delhi as the three most promising cities for opening physical coffee shops.

1. Pune – First Choice

Pune is my strongest recommendation because it generates the highest total sales and number of purchases.
Although it ranks third in unique customers, its high purchase activity suggests strong demand and repeat purchasing.
It also has the third-lowest rent per customer, making it an attractive option for expansion.

2. Chennai – Second Choice

Chennai is my second recommendation, with the second-highest total sales and number of purchases. 
It also has a strong customer base and moderate rent per customer. 
This combination makes Chennai a promising location for opening a physical coffee shop.

3. Delhi – Third Choice

Delhi is my third recommendation. Although it ranks fifth in total sales, it has a large customer base and ranks fourth in total purchases. 
Its moderate rent per customer also supports its potential as an expansion location.

Overall Conclusion

Pune offers the strongest combination of revenue, purchasing activity, and rental cost efficiency.
Chennai demonstrates strong overall sales performance, while Delhi offers an opportunity to build on its existing customer base.
These three cities should be prioritised for further market research before making a final investment decision.
